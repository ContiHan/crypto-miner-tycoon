Build-Matrix Balance — THE LAST SATOSHI

Sim-time for each class × keystone build to mine one full 21,000,000 BTC supply (the win = the Back-in-Time target), driving the real GameLogic with aggressive rig/research reinvestment. Lighter = faster win, deeper blue = slower grind. Generated by test/build_matrix_sim_test.dart.

What the matrix says
  • Class balance is tight. With no keystone, the four classes + Prospector win in 1h06–1h18 — a 1.18× spread. No class is a trap or a runaway; Corporation edges it via +hash/+income, Prospector trails.
  • The keystone levers move throughput in the expected direction. ASIC Monoculture (+100% hash) is the fastest across the board (~40–50m); the rig-slam Junkyard Rigs and the pre-prestige "free power" of Paper Hands / Market Maker also win fast. The offline-parity Cold Wallet Discipline and Low Time Preference trade foreground income for their perk, so they grind slower.
  • The Power Bill rail holds. Net-kept stayed in [0.90, 1.0] for every one of the 65 builds; Furnace Farm pins it to exactly 0.90 (the 10% upkeep cap), nothing skims more.
  • No build breaks the economy. Zero NaN / Infinity / negative-wallet / prestige-runaway across the whole grid.

Read the DNF cells honestly. This is a pure-mining sim: it never opens a crate, plays SWEEP, fires a chaos event, or prestiges. So keystones whose upside lives in those loops show only their downside here — Degenerate Gambler (luck×2 for loot/SWEEP; the sim sees only hash/income×0.5) can't fill an era on the no-boost classes and reads DNF. Sweat Equity / Laser Eyes (click builds, under-tapped on purpose) and Paper Hands / Market Maker (prestige/chaos costs, not paid in a first-era sprint) are likewise mis-priced here. The matrix validates mining throughput + economic health, not full build power.
